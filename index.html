Overview

JPMAFJ is a personal wealth journal designed for investors, savers, and planners who want a calm, focused place to think about money — without giving up their data, their privacy, or their attention to advertising and brokerage upsells. It helps you record, organize, and analyze every part of your financial life entirely on your own device.

What You Can Do

Multi-Account Journal

Track checking, savings, credit, cash, and investment accounts side by side with multi-currency support.

Transactions & Receipts

Log every transaction with payee, category, tag, memo, and an attached receipt photo from camera or library.

Portfolio & Lots

Record individual lots for each holding and compute true average cost, cost basis, and unrealized P/L.

Budgets

Set monthly or custom periods, scoped to a category or full spend, with progress filled in as transactions land.

Goals

Plan toward a down payment, emergency fund, or your next adventure, with contribution tracking and progress rings.

Dashboard & Reports

Net-worth trend, scrubbable cash-flow bars, allocation donut, sparklines, and selectable time-range reports.

Privacy at a Glance

  • See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Tracking Declaration

JPMAFJ does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial data. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting libraries, no crash-reporting services, and no remote logging. The app makes no network calls related to your data. All identifiers, instruments, transactions, and receipts stay on the device you use.

The only information that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to send us — for example, an email you write to datltan1991@icloud.com when you contact support.
