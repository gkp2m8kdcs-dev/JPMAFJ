Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Does JPMAFJ track me?

No. JPMAFJ contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. See the Privacy Policy for the full Tracking Declaration.

Can I back up or move my data?

Yes. Use the in-app "Export Backup" action to save a JSON snapshot to the Files app, AirDrop, or any share destination. You can re-import the same JSON on another device.

How do I delete all of my data?

Open the app and use the "Wipe All Data" action, or simply uninstall the app. Both remove all on-device data. The action is immediate and cannot be undone — export first if you might want a copy.

What do the dashboard charts show?

Net-worth trend, monthly cash flow (income vs. expenses), allocation by category and by asset, sparklines on accounts and holdings, plus quick stats for savings rate and goal progress. All charts are computed locally from your data.

How is portfolio cost basis calculated?

Each holding tracks individual lots (date, quantity, price per unit). Average cost is the weighted average across lots; unrealized P/L is based on the most recent price you entered.

Is the app investment advice?

No. Charts, ratios, and signals are educational and informational only and do not constitute investment, tax, or legal advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Which permissions does the app ask for?

Only Camera and Photo Library — and only when you tap "Attach Photo" on a transaction. No location, contacts, microphone, or background permissions are requested.

Troubleshooting

I can't attach a photo

Open iOS Settings → JPMAFJ and confirm that Photos and/or Camera access is allowed. If you previously denied access, you will need to grant it there before the in-app photo picker will work.

My charts look empty

Add at least two transactions on different dates, or use the in-app "Load Demo Data" action to explore the app with sample data.

My portfolio P/L looks wrong

Enter the most recent price per unit on the holding screen. P/L is computed against the price you set and the date you choose.
