Privacy Policy

JPMAFJ — Your Private Wealth & Investment Journal
Last updated: 2026-10-19

Summary

JPMAFJ does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal or financial data. All accounts, transactions, holdings, budgets, goals, and receipt photos are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app is built with a strict no-tracking design. In particular, JPMAFJ does not collect:

  • Identifiers (no IDFA, no device ID, no advertising ID, no user ID).
  • Usage analytics, behavior events, screen views, taps, or session data.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics, or performance telemetry sent off-device.
  • Location data, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera roll (beyond the photo you explicitly choose).
  • Account, transaction, holding, budget, or goal data of any kind.
  • Receipt photos or any other file you store in the app.

2. Tracking Declaration

JPMAFJ does not use tracking technologies. The app contains:
  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Amplitude, no Segment, etc.).
  • No tracking pixels, beacons, or fingerprinting libraries.
  • No crash-reporting services (no Crashlytics, no Sentry, no Bugsnag, etc.).
  • No A/B testing or remote-config frameworks.
  • No third-party SDKs that perform tracking on behalf of the developer.
The app makes no network calls related to your data. The only outbound traffic originates from you — for example, the email you write when you contact datltan1991@icloud.com.

3. Information You Store

All information you enter into JPMAFJ — including account names, balances, transactions, payees, categories, tags, memos, holdings, lots, prices, budgets, goals, contributions, and attached receipt photos — is stored only on that device, inside the app's local storage.

You can export this data as a JSON backup snapshot, restore the bundled demo dataset, or permanently wipe everything using the in-app "Wipe All Data" action. Wiping is immediate and irreversible.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you tap "Attach Photo" and choose "Camera" to capture a receipt. The image is saved inside the app's storage and never uploaded.
  • Photo Library — used only when you tap "Attach Photo" and choose "Library" to pick an existing receipt. The selected image is imported into the app's storage and never uploaded.
  • No other permissions are requested. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, tracking, or background-access permissions.

5. Children

JPMAFJ is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we collect no information at all, this policy is satisfied by design.

6. International Users

JPMAFJ does not transfer your data internationally because it does not transfer your data at all. No data is sent to the developer, to service providers, or to any third party.

7. Your Rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on a server. On the device, you have full control at all times. You may:

  • Export a JSON backup of your data from inside the app.
  • Wipe all locally stored data from inside the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its on-device data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this same URL. Because we do not collect data, no notification is required for changes that do not affect already-collected data.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact datltan1991@icloud.com.
